Mic & camera test for calls

Sound and look your best on every call.

PreCall runs a one-minute check of your microphone, headset, speakers and camera, then tells you exactly what to fix: background noise, a muffled or “old phone line” Bluetooth mic, hum, static, dropouts, bad lighting and more.

Echo test call: record a sentence, get a 0–100 score, and hear yourself back.

Bluetooth check: detects when a headset drops into low-quality hands-free mode.

Camera check: lighting, backlight, focus, color and frame rate.

Private: everything is analyzed on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
