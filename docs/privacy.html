Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

PreCall (“the app”) is a microphone, speaker and camera test tool. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it. In short: PreCall does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the app accesses and why

  • Microphone: to record short test clips and measure audio quality (level, background noise, clarity, tone, hum, static, dropouts and echo).
  • Camera: to show a live preview, measure video quality (lighting, focus, color, frame rate), and record an optional 10-second video test clip.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: only to list connected headsets and switch the microphone to the Bluetooth headset you choose.

How your data is handled

  • All audio and video is processed on your device, in memory. Recordings are never uploaded, never sent to any server and never shared with anyone.
  • Test recordings are discarded when you leave the result screen. They are not saved to your phone’s storage or gallery.
  • Test results (scores and measurements such as “background noise: −62 dBFS”, plus the name of the microphone used) are saved in the app’s private storage on your device so you can see your history. You can delete individual results or your whole history in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. Results are excluded from cloud backups.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It never sends your audio, video or test results anywhere.

Children

PreCall is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
