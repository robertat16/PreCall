## Full description (≤4000)

```
Walk into every call sounding and looking your best. PreCall checks your microphone, headset, speakers and camera in about a minute, then tells you exactly what to fix.

ONE-MINUTE FULL CHECK
A guided pass through your mic, echo and camera, combined into one call-readiness score.

ECHO TEST CALL
Read a short sentence, then hear yourself back the way others will. You get a 0–100 score covering:
• Voice level and distortion (clipping)
• Background noise and voice-to-noise ratio
• Clarity, including detection of Bluetooth "hands-free" mode, the #1 reason headsets sound muffled on calls
• Tone balance (bass and treble compared with a natural voice)
• Electrical hum, static, crackle and dropouts
• Echo: whether your mic picks up your own speaker
Every result comes with a plain-English fix.

COMPARE HEADSETS
Test two headsets or mics and see them side by side, so you know which one to use.

LIVE MIC METER
A real-time level meter and frequency spectrum, perfect for finding the right mic position.

SPEAKER & HEADPHONE TEST
Left/right channel check, a bass tone and a rising sweep that shows how much of the range your speakers or headphones reproduce.

CAMERA CHECK
Analyzes lighting, backlight, contrast, focus, color, resolution and frame rate, with a framing guide.

VIDEO TEST CALL
Record a 10-second clip and watch it back exactly as others will see and hear you.

CHOOSE YOUR MIC
Switch between your Bluetooth headset, a wired or USB mic, and the phone's built-in mic.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything is processed on your device. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Recordings are never uploaded or saved; only scores are kept, and you can delete them any time.

Great before work calls, interviews, online classes, podcasts and gaming sessions.

Scores are guidance, not lab measurements.
```
